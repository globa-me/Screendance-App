# Screendance App 1.7.3 — development record

Release after the local-only 1.7.2, made on branch
`screendance/window-close-pause` on November 16, 2026 at the owner's request
(build, sign, install in `/Applications`, publish on GitHub). arm64 only.
1.7.3 also carries the 1.7.2 fixes (security B1–B5, export cancel B17; see
`docs/releases/v1.7.2.md`), which were never published on their own.

## Contents (commit `793c6975`)

- HUD "hide" with the floating camera preview visible folds only the control
  bar into a pill (`hudBarCollapsed`, `LaunchWindow.tsx`); main sends
  `hud-overlay-expand-bar` on tray/dock show. Without a preview the HUD window
  is minimized as before.
- Floating camera preview: S/M/L (128/192/288 px, localStorage
  `screendance.hud.webcamPreviewSize`) and collapse to a 44 px camera button.
- Webcam track `ended` during a take → `reconnectWebcamCamera` reopens the
  same device (20 × 1.5 s). Diagnosis of the 2026-11-16 Continuity Camera
  drop (wireless link, OS side) is in `AGENTS.md`.
- Presets no longer apply app-wide preferences (fresh-recording auto zooms,
  new-zoom defaults, last manual focus, Whisper paths).
- Timeline: "Remove all zooms" (one undo step, 8 s toast with Undo); short
  non-clip/audio items get a 44 px minimum hit width.
